Privacy Policy

Last updated: [fill in the date you publish this]

query.academy ("we", "the app") is a self-paced learning platform for SQL, Python, C#, JavaScript, Excel, Power BI, Cloud, Data Analytics, and API courses. This page explains what information the app and website collect, why, and how it's handled — in plain language, not legal boilerplate.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name — shown back to you in the app, so it doesn't feel anonymous
  • Your email address — used to identify your account and sign you in
  • Your password — never stored as plain text. It's run through a one-way hashing function before it ever touches the database, meaning even we can't see or recover your actual password

We don't collect your location, contacts, camera/microphone access, or any device identifiers beyond what's needed to keep you signed in.

Cookies and sessions

Once you sign in, a small session cookie is stored so you stay logged in between visits. It's used only for that — recognizing that a request is coming from your already-logged-in account. It's not used for advertising or cross-site tracking of any kind.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We don't access anything on your device beyond normal app storage/network access needed to run the app itself.

Where your data is stored

Account data is stored in a database on our own hosting, not a third-party data broker or analytics platform. It's only accessed to operate the features you're actually using — signing you in, showing your account details back to you.

The Android app specifically

The Android app is built from the same code as the website, and shares the same account system — signing in on one signs you in on the other using the same email and password. The app's course content is bundled inside it so lessons work offline; signing in, and anything that needs to reach our servers, still requires a connection.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for general audiences learning technical skills and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.

Your choices

You can stop using the app or website at any time. To request that your account and associated data be deleted, contact us using the details below — we'll process the request and confirm once it's done.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect or how we use it ever changes in a meaningful way, we'll update this page and change the "last updated" date at the top.
